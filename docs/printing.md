# Printing

Receipts are drawn on a canvas, packed into a 1-bit raster and sent as one ESC/POS
`GS v 0` image (`www/js/services/printer.js`). The owner wants raster printing only:
no printer fonts or code pages, so Hindi prints the same as English.

## Android: Bluetooth plugin

The APK talks to the printer through **`cordova-plugin-bluetooth-serial`** (Bluetooth
Classic, SPP/RFCOMM), wrapped by `cordovaTransport()` in `www/js/services/printer-link.js`.
`cordova-plugin-android-permissions` asks for Nearby devices on Android 12+: `BLUETOOTH_CONNECT`
and `BLUETOOTH_SCAN` (one dialog; SCAN is `neverForLocation`, so no location prompt). Both are
needed before any connect: the plugin's connect thread calls `cancelDiscovery()`, which throws
without SCAN and closes the app. `PrinterLink.canConnect` refuses the link until both are granted.
Capacitor runs Cordova plugins as they are.

Reports: `services/printer-telemetry.js` adds the step (scan, connect, print), the printer's
Bluetooth name, Android version and both permission states to Crashlytics (custom keys, logs, a
non-fatal report on each caught failure; crash reports are always on in the APK). A connect is marked on the
phone while it runs; if the app closes during it, the next start sends "App closed during printer
connect" to the `telemetry` collection (Diagnostics) too. No MAC address.

The website has no Bluetooth path (no Web Bluetooth / Web Serial transport): Settings says
"Printing works in the Android app", and Billing's printer button is not shown.

### Billing's printer button

`www/js/ui/printer-shortcut.js`: a button in the app bar next to the bell, on Billing and
Wholesale, only where `PrinterService.isAvailable()` (the plugin is there). Its dot is
`data-state` connected / connecting / saved / none, redrawn on `PRINTER_CHANGED`
(`utils/events.js`, emitted by `PrinterService.updateStatus()`). A tap puts the Settings page in
(`TemplateLoader.ensurePage('settings')`) and moves its `#printerPanel` into the `#printerSheet`
sheet; closing (✕, scrim, Esc, Back, or leaving the page) moves it back. One copy of the
panel, the same handlers, and the bill on the form is never touched.

### Check the printer (test page and photo)

In the printer panel (Settings › Printing & receipts, and so also in Billing's printer sheet),
`www/js/ui/printer-test.js`, imported on first use (service worker `LAZY_ASSETS`):

- **Connection check** (a list, refreshed with the printer status and after each test; **Check
  connection** asks again): printer chosen (name, paper width), Bluetooth on/off, Nearby devices
  (`BLUETOOTH_CONNECT` + `BLUETOOTH_SCAN`; "not needed" below Android 12), connected now, and the
  last print's time and result (this phone, `localStorage.printerLastTest`, or a newer print this session).
- **Print a test page**: one line in the printer's own font (`Printer font: OK …`, ASCII only),
  then a picture: shop name, date and time, app version and build, printer and paper width, a line
  of English and one of Hindi, a 1 mm ruler numbered every 10 mm, a QR code, "Test OK". Printer
  fonts have no Devanagari, so Hindi only prints in image mode (as every bill does); the font line
  shows whether text mode works at all.
- **Print a photo**: Take photo / Choose photo in the app (file inputs, as photo of weights; no
  camera plugin). The preview is what prints: fitted to the paper width (384 dots for 58 mm, 576
  for 80 mm; the printer's width, changeable here), at most 1,200 dots tall (a taller photo is made
  smaller), dithered to black and white (Atkinson, or Floyd–Steinberg) with brightness and contrast.
  **Print** shows a progress line and **Stop printing**.

Bytes (`www/js/utils/escpos-image.js`, pure, `__tests__/escpos-image.test.js`): `ESC @ ESC a 1`,
then `GS v 0` bands of whole rows of at most 1 KB each, then `ESC d 3 GS V A 3`. `PrinterLink.sendChunks`
writes the chunks one at a time with 60 ms between them (cheap printers have small buffers), as one
job in the print queue; Stop between chunks sends only `ESC d 3` (feed), so the printer is never left
inside an image. Each chunk is its own `Uint8Array`: the plugin's `write` sends `data.buffer`, so a
view into a bigger buffer would send all of it. Every failure (no printer, permission, Bluetooth off,
connect, write, unreadable photo) shows a message and sends a Crashlytics non-fatal through
`PrinterTelemetry.error(step)` with the `printer_*` keys (`test_page`, `test_photo`, `test_page_draw`,
`test_photo_pick`, `test_photo_load`, `test_photo_render`, `test_check`).

The two Capacitor printer plugins (`@capacitor-community/bluetooth-le`,
`capacitor-thermal-printer`) and the `escpos-buffer` package were never used by the app
and were removed (#135). `android/app/capacitor.build.gradle` and
`android/capacitor.settings.gradle` are generated by `npx cap sync android`; commit them
whenever a plugin is added or removed, so a sync leaves no diff.

### WhatsApp: the bill as its receipt picture

Every WhatsApp button on a bill (Billing and Sales "Share draft", Wholesale, History › bill ›
Share, the "Last saved" strip) sends the **receipt picture**, not text
(`services/bill-image-share.js`).
- **One layout:** the picture is drawn by the printer's own receipt drawing
  (`receipt-drawing.js` `_renderBillCanvas` → `_drawBill`, with the same `receiptOptions()`: footer,
  QR, receipt language, logo). A change to the slip changes the picture too; a test pins the same
  lines at the same places for the same bill. It is the 58 mm layout drawn at 2× (768 px plus a
  white margin), in grey, not turned into printer dots. Hindi uses the same font path as the print.
  A draft carries the "DRAFT, not saved" mark; a reprint carries COPY.
- **Caption** with the picture: title, bill number, total and the due when there is one.
- **Android app:** the PNG goes to the app's cache (`@capacitor/filesystem`, `bill-share/`), then
  `AppShell.shareToWhatsApp` (`AppShellPlugin.java`) sends it with `ACTION_SEND` to WhatsApp, or
  WhatsApp Business (Android asks which when both are installed). With the customer's number
  (a picked contact's "Name - Number", or a stored phone) it adds `jid` = `<91…>@s.whatsapp.net`,
  so WhatsApp opens that chat with the picture attached; without a number WhatsApp asks whom to
  send it to. `jid` is not a documented WhatsApp API: when WhatsApp ignores it, it still asks whom.
  WhatsApp not installed, or an APK before rc22: Android's share sheet (`@capacitor/share`).
- **Browser:** Web Share with the file; without it, the picture is downloaded and WhatsApp opens
  with the text.
- **Share as text:** press and hold (right-click on a computer) any of these WhatsApp buttons. If
  the picture can't be made or shared, the text goes by itself with a toast ("The bill picture could
  not be shared, so the bill went as text."); closing the share sheet sends nothing. The text goes
  to the customer's chat (`wa.me/<number>`) when the bill has the number. Text format: dates
  "25 Nov 2026, 12:14 am", lines "₹55.00 × 20 kg = ₹1,100" (rate with paise, whole-rupee
  amounts, MONEY-TEXT).

### Cordova or Capacitor for Bluetooth? (decision, #135)

**Stay on the Cordova plugin for now.**

| | Cordova `bluetooth-serial` (current) | Capacitor options |
|---|---|---|
| Printer type | Classic SPP: what the cheap 58/80 mm shop printers speak | `bluetooth-le` is BLE only; most of these printers have no BLE, or a vendor-specific BLE service with 20–512 byte writes (slow, per-model) |
| Works today | Yes: connect, write, list, isConnected, drop callback | `capacitor-thermal-printer` (0.2.x) is young, little used, and would replace our raster path with its own |
| Maintenance | Unmaintained since 2017, but small and stable; Android 12+ permissions are asked by the app | Maintained |
| Cost to switch | — | A custom Capacitor plugin (`BluetoothSocket` RFCOMM: list / connect / write / isConnected, about 150 lines of Java) plus a new transport in `printer-link.js` |

The switch is not trivial and buys nothing today. Revisit it when one of these happens:
- a Capacitor upgrade drops Cordova plugin support;
- a newer Android or `targetSdk` breaks connect or write on the shop's phones;
- the shop moves to a BLE-only printer.

Then write the small RFCOMM plugin under `android/` and add a transport with the same
shape as `cordovaTransport()` (`available`, `canCheck`, `connect(id, onDrop)`, `disconnect`,
`isConnected`, `write(bytes)`, `list`); `PrinterLink` and everything above it stay as they are.
