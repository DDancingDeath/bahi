# Today

Part of the [owner guide](../owner-guide.md) (one file per page). Hindi: [today.hi.md](today.hi.md). The codes in brackets are spec IDs in [calculations.md](../calculations.md).

The owner opens the app on Today (managers and members open on Billing).

Today's figures, from today's bills, expenses and cash sessions.

The page has four groups, top to bottom. The app bar shows "Today" with the date ("Mon, 23 Nov") and the bell.

- **Money now** (no heading): the net figure for the day (T12) with **New bill** at its right, total in / out (T10, T11; green ↙ in, indigo ↗ out) with a bar under them that shows in and out as its two shares (hidden while both are ₹0). Tap **Total in** or **Total out** for the cash and online split (T6–T9). Then one line **Cash in drawer · since 8:45 am · ₹9,312 ›** (C7: what should be in the drawer now; it opens Today › Cash). With no drawer open today it reads **Drawer not open** with **Open cash**; after closing it reads **Counted** and the count in colour: "₹62 short" in red, "₹40 extra" in amber, or ✓ Matches in green (C8). It uses the day's cash session the phone already has (no read of its own). Then four tiles, the count on the label line ("Purchases · 10 ›", "Sales · 10 ›", "Business exp. · 4 ›", "Personal exp. · 1 ›"; T1, T2, T4, T5). Tap **Purchases** or **Sales** for how that day's bills were paid (T18): Cash, Online, Paid later today and Due (and Written off when there is some); Sales shows Retail and Wholesale as two short blocks. The expense tiles open Expenses.
- **Needs attention**: for owners and managers the **Day report**, then **Reminders**, each one line. The Day report line shows "No issues" or the counts in their colours ("High 2 · Review 3"), without the date (it is today's card), an amber dot when something needs a look, and one share icon at the right (WhatsApp text or an image; the share text keeps the date, the counts and the top items and parties), usable while the line is closed. When an app check is below its target today, the line also has "App health 1"; opened, it lists the checks with **Open app health**. Opened, the Day report shows at most 3 checks to review, each one line with **Looks right** and ⋯, then **All anomalies ›** with the number still to review. It opens by itself **once that day** when there is a High anomaly, an app health alert or a short / extra count after the drawer is closed; after that the line stays as you leave it. The Reminders line shows a short chip per reminder (for example "Dues ₹265 · Backup"; it ends in "…" when it doesn't fit) with an amber dot when something is overdue or late, and **＋** at the right to add one. When there is nothing to remind, owners and managers see only the line with **＋**. Rentals items are not on this Today: rent due and the rent agreements and increases (REM-2, REM-4) are in the Needs attention row at the top of the Rentals home (the bell and the phone notifications stay).
- **Settle today**: **Labour**, one line "Deducted ₹252 · Est. pay ₹366"; opened: Deducted, Estimated pay and Paid, with **Details ›** for each bill and the working. No line says which is more: the numbers say it. **Dues today**, one line "Get ₹4,320 · Pay ₹34,436" (new dues made today, in amber); opened: Collected (green ↙), Paid off (indigo ↗), New to collect, New to pay, and **Open dues ›**.
- **Details**: **Items**, one line "10 bought · 6 sold"; opened: one table per bill type (Purchase, Retail sale, Wholesale sale, each with its number of bills): Item | kg | ₹/kg | Total, one line per item (a long name ends in "…"), highest total first, with a small **×N** when the item is on more than one bill of that type. There is no total row: item lines are before labour, so they don't add up to the tile. Then one row **Today's bills · N ›**; it opens History on Today for that visit (the bills are listed there).

Every line below the tiles is one row: the title at the left, the key number at the right, then a tool if it has one. Day report, Reminders, Labour, Dues today and Items start **closed**; tap a line to open it and again to close it; this phone remembers each one. Pull down to refresh: the figures are drawn again and the Day report is scanned again.

On a computer 1280 px and wider the page has two columns: on the left the net strip, the four tiles in one row, Items (the tables always open) and Today's bills; on the right Cash in drawer, Needs attention, Labour and Dues today (always open, as panels with their title on the top edge) and **Cash and online** (cash in, online in, cash out, online out). There is no pull there: the refresh button sits left of the bell.

| On screen | How it is worked out |
|---|---|
| Purchases, Sales (T1, T2) | Sum of today's bill totals, with the number of bills on the label line. |
| Cash in drawer (C7, C8) | Open: the drawer's opening + cash added + cash in − cash out, as Today › Cash shows it. Closed: the saved count against what was expected. |
| Business / personal expenses (T4, T5) | Today's expenses of each kind, cash and online. |
| Cash / online received (T6, T7) | Paid at save on today's sales + due payments received today. |
| Cash / online paid (T8, T9) | Paid at save on today's purchases + today's expenses + due payments paid today. |
| Total in, total out, net (T10–T12) | In = cash + online received; out = cash + online paid; net = in − out. |
| Dues today › Collected / Paid off (T13, T14) | Due payments received / paid today. |
| Dues today › Get / Pay: new to collect, new to pay (T15, T16) | What today's own sales / purchases still owe (not the total dues). The all-time totals are on Dues. |
| Items (T17) | Today's bill lines by item, one table per bill type (purchase, retail, wholesale): kg, ₹/kg (value ÷ kg) and total; ×N = on N bills of that type. |
| By bill type (T18), in the Purchases / Sales sheet | Each type's total split into cash and online paid at save, paid later today, written off and still due; these add up to the total. Cash and online (Total in / out) is different: it counts all cash and online money of the day, dues and expenses too. |
| Today's bills (T19) | The count of today's bills per type; the row opens History › Today. |
| Reminders: Collect udhaar, Collect wholesale dues, Pay customers (REM-1) | Parties with a bill **more than 30 days old** still owing: the headline is their whole dues; "of which overdue (> 30 days)" is the part on those old bills (OVERDUE, owner decision, 8 Nov 2026): Collect udhaar has the same ₹ as Dues › Sales › Retail "Overdue (> 30 days)", Pay customers as Dues › Purchases; Collect wholesale dues follows the Wholesale page's own list. Each party shows its whole due, "₹Y overdue" and the age of its oldest due. |
| Labour › Deducted (T-LAB-1) | Sum of the labour on today's purchase bills. Opened, Labour has three rows (Deducted, Estimated pay, Paid); **Details ›** opens each bill, the working of Estimated pay and the note that a part package counts as a full one; **History ›** (you and managers) opens Money › Labour: the same figures day by day (LH1). |
| Labour › Paid (T-LAB-4) | Today's business expenses of type Labour (also Labor, Labour charges, Labourers, or Labour and wages written in Hindi; any case): what the labourers were paid. Already in today's business expenses; shown here to compare. |
| Paid more / less than deducted (T-LAB-5) | Paid − deducted: the shop's own labour cost for the day. Today shows no line for it (compare Paid with Deducted); Money › Spending says it. Profit does not change (owner decision, 8 Nov 2026). |
| Labour › Estimated pay (T-LAB-2) | Today's purchase kg ÷ the package weight (Settings › Aadhat, default 35 kg), **rounded up**, × the labour rate. |
| Estimated pay vs deducted (T-LAB-3) | The gap between the two (no line on Today; compare the two rows). Not an error: it happens when labour was unticked on a bill or packets were light. |
| Reminders: To do (REM-5) | Your own reminders ("Collect from Ramesh", a date, a party). You and managers add them with **＋ Add reminder**; everyone sees the ones due today or late and the next 7 days, and anyone may tap **✓ Done** (it goes for everyone). **✎ Change** and **✕ Cancel** are for you and managers. A reminder may have a **time** (optional). With phone notifications on (Settings › Help & about › Notifications), the Android app shows it in the status bar at that time, 9:00 am without one, even with the app closed; the browser only while the app is open. |
| Repeating reminders (REM-6) | A reminder can repeat every day, week or month. When someone taps **✓ Done**, the next one is made at once, one period later (a late one moves to today or the next date after it, never several late copies); a monthly one keeps its day of the month (the 31st falls on the last day of shorter months). Set "Once" with **✎ Change** to stop it repeating. |
| Reminders: Salaries due (WP-11) | Owner only. A month's pay counts as due from the 1st of that month, so from the 1st it shows the workers still to be paid and the total left (the Workers / Salaries page's "Still to pay" for this month), as last worked out on this phone. **Open Workers / Salaries** opens the page. It shows once Workers / Salaries was opened on this phone; it reads nothing at start and is brought up to date once a day. |
| Reminders: Bills due (RB-11) | Owner only. When a recurring bill is due within the next 3 days or is overdue, it shows **Bills due: N, ₹X** (and how many have no amount entered yet), as last worked out on this phone. **Open Recurring bills** opens the page. It shows once Recurring bills was opened on this phone; it reads nothing at start and is brought up to date once a day. |
| Owner only: Waiting to join, written off by others, Set up your shop, Lock this phone | **N people waiting to join** (Approve opens Settings › Team with the waiting people; counted once a session); dues **written off this week by someone else** (from the bills on this phone, also in the bell); the **Set up your shop** list for a new shop (business name, items and rates, team, printer, with Help & about) until done or hidden; and once, **Lock this phone when not in use?** |
